Welcome to South Eastern Dreamers Cricket Club.

We are a passionate cricket club committed to fostering talent, teamwork and
sportsmanship. Whether you're a seasoned player or new to cricket, join us for
thrilling cricket. Dream big, play hard, and be part of our cricket family.

**Three teams. Two competitions. One club.**

## Where we play

Saturdays we're in the Eastern Cricket Association with two sides, LOC 1 (the
McCarthy Shield) and LOC 3 (the Reg Lamborn Shield). Sundays we're in the
Victorian Sunday Cricket Association in Grade 4.

## Getting involved

The playing group for 2026/27 is full across all three sides. If you'd like to
be considered for a future season, or you're interested in sponsoring the club,
use the contact form and someone will get back to you.
