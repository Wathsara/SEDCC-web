Welcome to South Eastern Dreamers Cricket Club.

We are a progressive, community-focused cricket club committed to fostering
excellence, inclusivity and participation across all demographics. From one team
in our first season to three today, the club has grown with passion, teamwork and
community at its heart.

**Three teams. Two competitions. One club.**

## Where we play

Saturdays we're in the Eastern Cricket Association with two sides, LOC 1 (the
McCarthy Shield) and LOC 2 (the Laws Shield). Sundays we're in the
Victorian Sunday Cricket Association in Grade 4. Our home ground is Singleton
Reserve, Endeavour Hills.

## Where we're going

Over the five years to 2030 we aim to consolidate our position as a leading
sporting organisation: investing in player development, expanding pathways for
juniors and women, improving our facilities, and strengthening governance and
community engagement. Read the full [strategic plan](/plan/).

## Getting involved

The senior playing group for 2026/27 is full across all three sides. The club is
growing though — four senior teams by 2030, a full junior pathway, and two
women's teams by 2028. If you'd like to be part of that as a player, coach,
volunteer or sponsor, use the contact form and someone will get back to you.
