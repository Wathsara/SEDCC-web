Dreamers Cricket Club will field three senior sides in 2026/27.

**Saturdays** we're in the Eastern Cricket Association with two teams, LOC 1 and
LOC 3. **Sundays** we're back in the Victorian Sunday Cricket Association in
Grade 4.

Fixtures aren't out yet. Associations usually publish grades and draws in
September, and everything on this site — every fixture, ladder and scorecard —
updates automatically once they do.

## Squads are set

All three sides are full. The club has grown from a single VSCA outfit in its
first season to three sides across two associations in as many years, and the
playing group for 2026/27 is finalised.

If you want to be considered for next season, get in touch closer to the end of
this one.

## What's new on the site

Every Monday morning through the season this site pulls the weekend's results
from PlayHQ and posts the top batter and top bowler across all three sides. No
one has to remember to update it.
